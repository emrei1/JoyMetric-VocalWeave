JoyMetric Virtual Audio Driver development framework

The Windows WDM/WaveRT skeleton is derived at build time from Microsoft's
Windows-driver-samples / audio/simpleaudiosample repository (MS-PL). The
upstream source is downloaded from Microsoft during the development build and
patched locally by JoyMetric tooling. The JoyMetric patch implements the custom
render-to-capture PCM ring transport, endpoint identity, and 32-bit stereo wire
format used by JoyMetric.

Upstream: https://github.com/microsoft/Windows-driver-samples
